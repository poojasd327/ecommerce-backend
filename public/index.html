⚡ How This Works Under the Hood

1

Step 1: Order Creation

Clicking "Pay Now" calls POST /api/payment/create-order on your Node.js backend. The server creates an order on Razorpay and returns an order_id.

2

Step 2: Razorpay Modal Popup

The client opens Razorpay's secure checkout modal with your public RAZORPAY_KEY_ID and the generated order_id.

3

Step 3: HMAC Signature Verification

Upon payment completion, Razorpay returns razorpay_payment_id and razorpay_signature. The client sends these to POST /api/payment/verify where your Node.js server verifies the HMAC-SHA256 signature.
